# Secure Data Collaboration

PryvX is delivered as **PET-as-a-Service**: a managed platform that lets regulated organizations — banks, healthcare providers, telecom operators, and government agencies — work together on shared intelligence and decision-making without exposing raw data to one another or to PryvX itself.

## What it enables

- Cross-institution collaboration on fraud, risk, and threat signals without bilateral data-sharing agreements for every pairing.
- Regulator-facing evidence that no raw personal or commercially sensitive data left its owner's control.
- Reuse of the same secure workspace for multiple analyses over time, instead of a one-off data export per project.

## How it works

Every collaboration runs inside a [confidential clean room](/overview/confidential-clean-rooms): each party connects its data in place, a contract defines exactly what computation may run and what output may be released, and the platform enforces that contract technically rather than relying on trust alone. See [How PryvX Works](/overview/how-pryvx-works) for the full flow, and [Creating a Workspace](/how-to-guide/creating-a-workspace) to set one up.
